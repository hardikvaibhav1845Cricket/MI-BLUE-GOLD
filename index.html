The basics

Know Mumbai Indians.

Mumbai Indians were formed for the inaugural IPL in 2008. The franchise represents Mumbai and plays its home matches at Wankhede Stadium. The team is owned by Indiawin Sports Pvt. Ltd., a Reliance group company. MI's IPL identity is built around blue and gold, the #OneFamily idea, high-performance scouting and a long-term approach to building squads.

🏟️

Home

Wankhede Stadium, Mumbai
One of the most iconic cricket grounds in India and the home of the Paltan.

👑

Ownership

Indiawin Sports Pvt. Ltd.
Mumbai Indians is owned by Reliance Industries through its wholly owned subsidiary IndiaWin Sports. Nita Ambani and Akash Ambani are publicly identified as team owners.

🧠

The MI way

Back talent early, build strong combinations, develop players, plan for cycles rather than one season, and keep competing even after setbacks.

People behind the franchise

Owners, leadership & ecosystem

Nita Ambani

Team owner and a prominent face of the Mumbai Indians organisation. She has been closely associated with MI's journey from its early years and has also championed grassroots sports initiatives through Reliance Foundation.

Akash Ambani

Team owner and part of the leadership around squad-building, auctions and the franchise's wider cricket operations. His role reflects MI's emphasis on long-term team cycles.

Reliance & Indiawin Sports

MI's ownership sits within the Reliance sports ecosystem. The organisation has expanded the Mumbai Indians brand into multiple franchise leagues and development initiatives.

2008 → 2026

From a new franchise to a dynasty.

The story was not an instant success. MI missed the playoffs in several early seasons, lost its first IPL final in 2010, then built a culture around smart recruitment, player development and calm pressure cricket.

2008
The beginning.
MI entered the first IPL season. Sanath Jayasuriya led the team in runs with 514, while Ashish Nehra took 12 wickets. Sachin Tendulkar was the icon player.
2009
Building the core.
The season was played in South Africa. Lasith Malinga made his MI debut; JP Duminy was the leading run-scorer with 372 and Malinga took 18 wickets.
2010
First IPL final.
Sachin Tendulkar produced a legendary season: 618 runs and the Orange Cap. MI reached its first IPL final but finished runners-up.
2011
The first major silverware.
MI won the Champions League T20. Lasith Malinga took 28 IPL wickets and won the Purple Cap. Rohit Sharma joined MI and the long-term core began taking shape.
2012
More heartbreak, more learning.
MI reached the playoffs. Rohit Sharma led the team in runs with 433; Malinga took 22 wickets.
2013
MI-racle at last.
Rohit took over the captaincy during the season. MI beat CSK in the IPL final to win its first IPL trophy, then won its second CLT20 title.
2014
The comeback qualification.
After a difficult start, MI stormed into the playoffs with a dramatic late-season run. Lendl Simmons led the batting and Malinga the bowling.
2015
Title number two.
MI recovered from a poor beginning, won 9 of its last 10 league matches and defeated CSK in the final. Lendl Simmons led the team with 540 runs; Malinga took 24 wickets.
2016
A reset.
MI finished fifth. Rohit Sharma led the runs with 489, while Mitchell McClenaghan took 17 wickets.
2017
Title number three.
MI won the IPL final by one run in a tense finish. The season also featured a 146-run win over Delhi, then the biggest winning margin in IPL history.
2018
Another reset.
MI finished fifth. Suryakumar Yadav's return season produced 512 runs; Hardik Pandya took 18 wickets.
2019
Title number four.
MI defeated CSK in a one-run final. Quinton de Kock scored 529 runs; Jasprit Bumrah took 19 wickets. MI also became the first team to 100 IPL wins.
2020
The fifth star.
In the UAE, MI won back-to-back titles. Ishan Kishan scored 516 runs and Bumrah took 27 wickets. MI became the first team to play 200 IPL matches.
2021
Defending the standard.
MI finished fifth. Rohit Sharma led the batting with 381 runs and Bumrah led the bowling with 21 wickets.
2022
A painful rebuild.
MI finished tenth. Ishan Kishan scored 418 runs and Bumrah took 15 wickets. Tilak Varma emerged as a major young talent.
2023
Young energy returns.
MI reached the playoffs. SKY scored 605 runs and Piyush Chawla took 22 wickets. Akash Madhwal's 5/5 in the Eliminator became a defining moment.
2024
A tough season, huge moments.
MI finished tenth. Rohit Sharma led the runs with 417; Bumrah took 20 wickets and recorded 5/21. Hardik Pandya returned to the Blue and Gold.
2025
A comeback season.
MI reached the playoffs after a six-match winning streak. Suryakumar Yadav led with 717 runs and Trent Boult took 22 wickets. The wider MI family also celebrated major overseas success.
2026
Keep building.
MI finished the IPL season with 4 wins from 14 matches. Ryan Rickelton led the team with 448 runs and Allah Ghazanfar took 15 wickets. The next chapter is still being written.
Silverware

Every major MI trophy chapter.

The trophies are the headline. The real story is the work between them: rebuilding, losing finals, changing combinations, trusting young players and returning stronger.

2011

Champions League T20 🏆

MI won its first major international club trophy, defeating RCB in the final. This was a foundation moment for the championship culture.

2013

IPL Champions 🏆

After the 2010 final defeat and two playoff exits, MI finally broke through. The first IPL trophy came against CSK at Eden Gardens.

2013

CLT20 Champions 🏆

MI defeated Rajasthan Royals to win its second Champions League T20 title, becoming the first team to win the competition twice.

2015

IPL Champions 🏆

A remarkable turnaround: after a poor start, MI won 9 of its final 10 league games and beat CSK again in the final.

2017

IPL Champions 🏆

A one-run final victory completed a dramatic campaign. MI's depth and late-over composure became defining traits.

2019

IPL Champions 🏆

MI defeated CSK by one run in a classic final. Malinga's final-over finish became one of the franchise's most famous endings.

2020

IPL Champions 🏆

MI dominated the UAE campaign and beat Delhi Capitals in the final to win its fifth IPL title and go back-to-back.

2023

WPL Champions 🏆

Mumbai Indians won the inaugural Women's Premier League title, adding a new chapter to the Blue-and-Gold story.

2025

WPL Champions 🏆

The women's team lifted its second WPL title. Nat Sciver-Brunt won the Orange Cap and MVP; Amelia Kerr won the Purple Cap.

The Paltan's greats

Star players & legends.

Not a ranking — a celebration of players who shaped different eras of Mumbai Indians.

Legend • Batter

Sachin Tendulkar

MI icon player, captain and the face of the early era. He won the 2010 IPL Orange Cap with 618 runs and was part of the 2013 title-winning side.

Legend • Captain

Rohit Sharma

Joined MI in 2011 and became captain in 2013. He led MI to all five IPL titles and became the central figure of the franchise's golden era.

Legend • Bowler

Lasith Malinga

MI's iconic yorker specialist. He won the 2011 IPL Purple Cap with 28 wickets and became a symbol of MI's death bowling.

Legend • All-rounder

Kieron Pollard

A long-term match-winner, finisher and leader. His calm power hitting became a major part of MI's title-winning identity.

Era-defining • Bowler

Jasprit Bumrah

Discovered and developed by MI. He became the franchise's modern bowling spearhead and produced the 27-wicket 2020 season.

All-rounder • Leader

Hardik Pandya

Rose through MI's system, became a title-winning all-rounder, later returned to the franchise and took over the captaincy.

Batter • SKY

Suryakumar Yadav

Returned to MI in 2018 and developed into one of the world's most explosive T20 batters. He topped MI's batting in 2023 and 2025.

WK-Batter

Ishan Kishan

One of MI's most explosive young batters. He led MI's scoring in the 2020 title season with 516 runs.

WK-Batter

Quinton de Kock

Key opener in the 2019 and 2020 championship teams. He scored 529 runs in MI's 2019 title-winning campaign.

Young Core • Batter

Tilak Varma

A major product of MI's talent pipeline who emerged from the 2022 rebuild into a key part of the team's future.

Bowler • Left-arm

Trent Boult

A vital part of the 2019–20 bowling combination. He returned to MI's squad in 2026 and led the team in wickets in 2025 with 22.

Legend • Bowler

Harbhajan Singh

Part of MI from the inaugural era and captain in 2011–12. He led MI to the 2011 Champions League T20 title.

The prime years

Peak MI was more than a lineup.

Elite bowling

Malinga's yorkers, Bumrah's evolution, Boult's new-ball threat and deep bowling options gave MI multiple ways to win.

Finishing power

Pollard and Hardik provided the ability to change games late, while Rohit and the top order controlled the middle overs.

Player development

Bumrah, Hardik, Krunal, SKY and Tilak are examples of players who grew into bigger roles after entering the MI ecosystem.

Calm under pressure

Several MI titles were decided by tiny margins. The team's reputation was built on staying composed when the game became chaotic.

Big-game culture

MI repeatedly turned playoff experience into lessons, especially after the 2010 final and the 2011–12 playoff exits.

Long-term thinking

MI's leadership has often talked about building for a cycle rather than chasing one season, accepting difficult squad changes when needed.

The rivalry

MI × CSK — the clash of eras.

A rivalry can be intense without disrespect. Mumbai Indians and Chennai Super Kings have met in some of the biggest IPL moments, especially finals and playoffs.

MI
Blue & Gold

Five IPL titles in the Rohit-led era and a long history of big-match wins.

VS
CSK
Yellow Army

One of the IPL's most successful teams, with legendary players and a huge global following.

The rivalry works because both teams have created unforgettable cricket. Finals in 2013, 2015 and 2019 are especially important chapters in MI history — but greatness in one team does not require disrespecting the other.
Respect the game

One league. Many stories.

Every IPL team has fans, legends, setbacks and unforgettable moments. This archive celebrates Mumbai Indians while respecting every franchise, every player, every supporter and the game itself.
